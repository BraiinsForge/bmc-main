# Fixture artwork provenance

None of this reaches a device. A deck fetches its imagery from the deployment; these files exist so the gallery scenes
and `bmc-netsim` have something deterministic to draw.

- **flags/** — rasterised from [Circle Flags](https://github.com/HatScripts/circle-flags), MIT. Terms and the rendering
  steps are in [`flags/LICENSE-CIRCLE-FLAGS`](flags/LICENSE-CIRCLE-FLAGS).
- **headshots/** — generated with Google Gemini. The faces are invented and depict nobody; they are numbered by grid
  slot rather than named for exactly that reason.
- **logos/** — AI-generated invented marks, numbered like the headshots and for the same reason: they represent no real
  constructor, and which team draws which number is arbitrary.
- **circuit.png** — LLM generated & rasterised.
